What Makes a Great Email Subject Line?

Your email subject line is the single most important factor in whether your email gets opened. Research from Mailchimp shows that subject lines between 6–10 words generate the highest open rates, while HubSpot data confirms that personalized subject lines outperform generic ones by up to 26%. Using this free email subject line tester, you can check all the key factors before you hit send.

A great subject line balances length, clarity, emotional resonance, and deliverability. Too short and it feels vague. Too long and it gets cut off on iPhone (after 41 characters) and Gmail mobile (after 40 characters). Our subject line checker scores your draft against all these dimensions instantly, with no signup required.

Email subject line best practices have evolved significantly. Spam filters have become more sophisticated — words like "free", "guaranteed", and "act now" still trigger filters in 2024. Numbers and questions consistently lift open rates across B2B and B2C. Personalization with the recipient's first name can increase open rates by 10–14% according to Campaign Monitor data.
