Professional Apology Email

GmailEmpathetic ToneTone Shift
Before (Original)
Hi, I wanted to reach out about the delay with your order. There were some unforeseen circumstances on our end that were outside of our control, including supplier issues and a system outage that affected many of our customers. We tried our best to resolve things quickly but unfortunately the situation was complicated. I hope you understand.
After (TextGlow Rewrite)Empathetic
Hi James, I'm sorry for the delay — this fell short of the experience we aim to provide. Your order will ship by Friday, and I've added a $20 credit to your account for the inconvenience. I'll send a tracking number as soon as it's in transit.

What Changed

  • 1Removed the defensive explanation chain that shifts blame to external factors
  • 2Led with a direct, clean apology (no "I wanted to reach out about")
  • 3Added concrete resolution details (date + credit) instead of vague reassurance

Why Empathetic tone works here

Empathetic tone in apologies focuses on the other person's experience first, then action — not on explaining yourself.

About Empathetic Tone

Prioritizes the reader's emotional experience. Validates before solving. Builds trust in tense situations.
