How to Use Textglow

Textglow is your instant AI writing assistant. It works directly inside Gmail, Slack, LinkedIn, and almost anywhere else you type on the web. It fixes grammar, improves clarity, and changes the tone of your text without any copy-pasting.

Getting Started in 3 Steps

1. Install & Pin

Add Textglow to Chrome from the Web Store. Once installed, we recommend pinning it to your browser toolbar for easy access.

Install Extension

2. Type Your Text

Open any website (like Gmail or LinkedIn) and type your draft. You don't need to be perfect—just get your ideas down.

3. Rewrite & Refine

Click the Textglow icon to open the rewriting menu.

  • Choose a Tone (your default tone is pre-selected).
  • Click Rewrite.
  • The menu closes and your text is instantly replaced by the AI-improved version. It's like magic!

One-Click Rewrite

Pro

The ultimate productivity hack.

Enable One-Click Rewrite in settings to bypass all menus.

  • No menus. No need to open any windows.
  • No selecting. Just click the ✨ Sparkle Icon in the corner of your text field.
  • Instant magic. Your draft is replaced with the rewritten version in a single click.
