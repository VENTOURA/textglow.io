What's One-Click Rewrite?

With the free plan, you click the ✨ icon and choose from 10 tones — Professional, Friendly, Confident, and more. It takes 2 clicks and about 3 seconds.

With One-Click Rewrite, you set your favorite default tone. Then, simply click the ✨ icon and TextGlow instantly rewrites your text using your preferred tone.

One click. Perfect tone. No menu. No waiting.

When you're writing dozens of messages a day, skipping the menu every time saves you valuable focus and keeps you in your flow.
